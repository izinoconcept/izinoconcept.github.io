Glossary

Plain-English definitions of terms used in our guides.

CAC
The Corporate Affairs Commission, the registrar of companies in Nigeria.
Capitation
A payment model in which a fixed amount is paid per covered person, regardless of how often that person uses the service.
DPR (defunct)
The former Department of Petroleum Resources, scrapped in 2021. Its regulatory functions passed to NUPRC and NMDPRA.
ECA 2010 / Employees' Compensation Scheme
The Employees' Compensation Act 2010 sets up a scheme, run by the NSITF, that compensates employees for work-related injury, disease or death. Employers contribute 1% of total monthly payroll.
Fee-for-service
A payment model in which each service is billed separately at an agreed price.
Fitness for work
A doctor's conclusion on whether a person can safely do a particular job. See our guide to medical examinations.
HMO
Health Maintenance Organisation: an organisation that arranges and pays for healthcare through a network of providers in return for a premium.
HSE
Health, Safety and Environment, the policies and practices that protect people and the environment at work.
ITF
The Industrial Training Fund, which collects training contributions from liable employers.
NCDMB
The Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board, which oversees local-content requirements in the oil and gas industry.
NMDPRA
The Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority, regulator for midstream and downstream petroleum operations.
NRS
The Nigeria Revenue Service, which replaced the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) on 1 January 2026.
NSITF
The Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund, which administers the Employees' Compensation Scheme.
NUPRC
The Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission, regulator for upstream petroleum operations.
Occupational health
The branch of healthcare concerned with workers' health in relation to their work, including examinations and exposure monitoring.
PenCom
The National Pension Commission, regulator of the pension system. Compliance evidence is often requested in tender pre-qualification.
Pre-authorisation
Approval, given before treatment, that a service will be covered and paid for.
Referral
Sending a patient from one provider to another, such as a specialist or a tertiary hospital.
Retainership
An arrangement in which a provider is engaged on agreed terms to serve a client over a period. See our guide.
Service-level agreement (SLA)
A written commitment on how quickly and how well a service will be delivered, such as response times.
TIN
Tax Identification Number.
Utilisation report
A periodic summary of how much a client's staff used a provider's services, usually reported in aggregate.

Definitions are simplified for general understanding. Check the relevant agency or a qualified adviser for the current legal position.

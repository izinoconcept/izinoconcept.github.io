About Us

Building strong, durable working relationships between parties who share mutual commercial interests.

Who We Are

i.zino concept is a Nigerian service and consultancy firm headquartered in Warri, Delta State. We focus on practical commercial outcomes: signed retainership agreements, improved customer-service systems, and clear documentation that helps businesses operate confidently.

Our work centres on introducing major clients—especially in the oil and gas sector—to hospitals and clinics for medical retainership arrangements, and on providing consultancy and customer-service support to organisations of every size.

What Guides Us

We believe lasting business relationships are built on clarity, reliability and genuine value for both sides. Whether we are facilitating a retainership contract or helping a company redesign how it handles enquiries and complaints, our aim is the same: results that clients can measure and trust.

Client Loyalty

We design services that turn first-time interactions into long-term relationships.

Local Knowledge

Deep understanding of the Nigerian business environment, especially oil & gas and healthcare.

Practical Delivery

We prioritise completed agreements, clear documentation and usable systems over theory.

Our Approach

Every engagement begins with listening. We take time to understand the operational needs of hospitals, the compliance requirements of energy companies, and the customer-experience goals of private businesses. From there we propose concrete next steps—introductions, draft agreements, process recommendations or documentation support—and stay involved until the work is complete.

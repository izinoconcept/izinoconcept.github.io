Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how i.zino concept uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember preferences and understand how visitors use the pages.

2. How We Use Cookies

We may use:

  • Essential cookies – required for basic site operation and security.
  • Analytics cookies – to understand traffic and improve the site (for example page views and approximate location).
  • Advertising cookies – where we display ads, third-party networks may set cookies to deliver and measure advertisements.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Advertising and analytics partners may set their own cookies. We do not control those cookies. Their use is governed by the respective third-party privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. You can usually find these controls in the browser’s settings or preferences menu. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

6. More Information

For details on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy. Questions can be sent via the Contact page.

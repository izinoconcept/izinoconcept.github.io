Cookie Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains how izinoconcept.github.io uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help a site work, remember preferences, measure usage and, where ads are shown, deliver and measure advertising.

2. Cookies and similar technologies on this site

PurposeProviderWhat it does
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics / Google Tag ManagerCounts visits and shows which pages are used, so we can improve the site. Data is aggregated; it does not tell us who you are.
AdvertisingGoogle AdSense (and Google's advertising partners)Delivers and measures ads, limits how often you see the same ad and, unless you opt out, may personalise ads based on your browsing.
FontsGoogle FontsLoads the typefaces used on the site. Your IP address is sent to Google when the fonts load.
Contact formFormSubmitDelivers messages you submit to our inbox.

We do not control third-party cookies. Their use is governed by the providers' own policies.

3. Managing your choices

  • Your browser. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how pages work.
  • Personalised ads. Opt out through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Analytics. Google provides a browser add-on that stops its analytics from collecting data about your visits.
  • Consent. Where the law requires consent for non-essential cookies, we ask for it and you can change your choice later.

4. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.

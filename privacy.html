Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information i.zino concept ("we", "us") collects through izinoconcept.github.io, how we use it, who we share it with and what rights you have. We are based in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria, and you can contact us at i.zinoconcept@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. If you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, organisation (optional), the subject and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically. When you visit, we and our service providers may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, time spent and approximate location. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy) and through server logs kept by our hosting provider.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve its content and performance.
  • To show advertising on the site and measure how it performs, as explained in section 4.
  • To keep the site secure and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.
  • Contact form: FormSubmit receives form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to measure traffic and usage.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense, described below.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts, which loads font files from Google's servers and therefore receives your IP address.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before cookies for personalised advertising are used.

5. Legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we process personal data where you have given consent (for example when you submit the contact form or accept cookies), where it is necessary to respond to your request, or where we have a legitimate interest in running and improving our website and where that interest is not overridden by your rights.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal data about you, to give you access to it, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email i.zinoconcept@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to deal with your request and for reasonable business and record-keeping purposes, after which we delete them. Analytics and advertising data are kept according to the settings and policies of the relevant providers.

8. International transfers and security

Some of our service providers, including Google, GitHub and FormSubmit, process data on servers outside Nigeria. We choose established providers and rely on their security measures, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

This site is aimed at businesses and professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Links to other sites

The site links to external websites such as Facebook and YouTube. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: i.zinoconcept@gmail.com, or use our contact page.

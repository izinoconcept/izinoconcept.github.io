Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

i.zino concept (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us (name, email address, organisation name and the content of your message). We may also collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited through server logs or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Information

We use contact details solely to respond to enquiries and to provide the services you request. Technical data helps us understand how the site is used and to improve it. We do not sell personal information.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, analytics and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (for example form handlers, analytics or advertising networks). Those providers process data according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable business or legal record-keeping purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details on our Contact page to make a request.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent version.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.

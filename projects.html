Projects & Portfolio

Examples of retainership work, registrations and client introductions we have supported.

Medical Retainership Agreements

Facilitated medical retainership and service-line agreements between oil & gas related companies and private medical centres. Examples include agreements involving Star Offshore Energy, BJ Bison Ventures and partner hospitals such as Anis Medical Centre and Hommies Medical Centre.

Focus: Clear service expectations, documentation and ongoing operational alignment.

Industry Registration Support

Assisted SMASA Nigeria Enterprises with successful registration at the Department of Petroleum Resources (DPR) to provide services to the oil industry, together with related NSITF employer registration and clearance documentation.

Focus: Regulatory readiness and formal market access.

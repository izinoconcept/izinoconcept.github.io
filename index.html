About i.zino concept

Based in Warri, Nigeria, i.zino concept specialises in building durable working relationships between parties who share mutual commercial interests.

Our core work involves introducing major clients in the oil and gas sector and corporate organisations to hospitals for medical retainership arrangements, and delivering practical consultancy and customer-service solutions that keep clients coming back.

We combine local knowledge with a clear focus on results: signed agreements, smoother service delivery and measurable client loyalty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about retainership, registration and how we work.

What does i.zino concept do?

We help organisations in three areas: customer-service operations, medical and business retainership introductions (connecting companies, particularly in oil and gas, with hospitals and clinics), and consultancy, including documentation support for industry registration.

Are you a hospital, an HMO or a law firm?

No. We facilitate introductions, agreement drafting support and documentation. Medical care is provided by the hospitals themselves, and formal legal advice should come from a lawyer qualified in Nigeria.

What is a medical retainership?

It is an agreement under which a company designates a hospital or clinic to look after its staff's healthcare needs on agreed terms. Read our guide to what a retainership agreement should cover.

How is a retainership different from HMO cover?

An HMO manages a network of hospitals and takes part of the financial risk in return for a premium. A retainership is a direct arrangement with a chosen hospital. We compare them in Medical Retainership or HMO?

How do I choose a hospital for a retainership?

Check licensing, emergency capacity, diagnostics, specialists, backup power and corporate billing, and visit in person. Our due-diligence checklist walks through each step.

Which regulator handles oil and gas service permits now?

The Department of Petroleum Resources (DPR) was scrapped in 2021. Its functions passed to the NUPRC (upstream) and the NMDPRA (midstream and downstream). Which one applies depends on your service category. See our registration checklist.

Do you guarantee that an application will be approved?

No. Approval decisions belong to the relevant agencies. We help you prepare accurate, complete documentation in the right order so that avoidable delays and repeat submissions are reduced.

How long does NSITF registration take?

NSITF indicates that a compliance certificate is typically processed within about five working days once all requirements are submitted and payment is verified. Your own timeline depends on how complete your documents are.

Where are you based and how do I contact you?

We are based in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. Email i.zinoconcept@gmail.com or use the form on our contact page.

What happens to the details I send you?

We use them to respond to your enquiry. See our Privacy Policy for how personal data is handled.
